About Me

Hi, I’m Krishna Shah, and I’m passionate about technology and helping others. At Household Haven, I oversee operations.

My journey in tech started with a love for solving problems and building things that can make life easier. Over time, I’ve worked on various projects and gained experience in both development and business operations. This led me to Household Haven, where I’m fortunate to be part of an amazing team.

As COO, my main role is to make sure everything runs smoothly. I focus on improving our processes, helping the team work better together, and making sure we deliver a great experience for our customers. It’s all about using technology to make things more efficient and help the company grow.

Outside of work, I enjoy learning about new technology, attending events, and mentoring others passionate about technology. I always seek new ways to learn and contribute to the company.

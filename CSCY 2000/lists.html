Descriptive List

This is a list of math equations that I learned so far in my life.

Pythagorean Theorem
The Pythagorean Theorem is used for solving the sides of a right triangle; of which are the adjacent, opposite, and hypotenuse sides.
Derivative
The derivative is used to find the instantaneous slope at a specific point.
Integral
The integral is used to find the area under a curve.
Difference of two squares
The difference of two squares is an equation that is equal to the sum of a and b & the difference of a and b.
Quadratic Formula
Used to find the x of any equation, and a, b, and c are given in a quadratic equation.
PEMDAS
PEMDAS is the basic numeric equations composed of: Parentheses, Exponents, Multiplication/Division, and Addition/Subtraction.
Distance Formula
The distance formula is used to calculate the distance between two points, in any graph.
